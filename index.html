02 — About

Learn by building.
Then build better.

No fake skills, no borrowed stories. Just a builder from Delhi who would rather ship a useful thing than talk about one.

I'm a student, creator, and builder from Delhi who learns by shipping.

My classroom is a browser tab. I start with a small annoyance — a file that's hard to edit, a tool that demands a login, a workflow that should be one click — and build the missing piece. Most of it ends up local-first, open source, and free to use.

I work at the intersection of AI-assisted development, open-source software, and Minecraft Bedrock. I'm not chasing frameworks or buzzwords. I care about whether a thing is fast, private, and genuinely useful when someone opens it.

Right now I'm sharpening my programming fundamentals, exploring local AI workflows, and growing The Antigle into a family of tools people can rely on.

"Useful beats impressive. Every time."
